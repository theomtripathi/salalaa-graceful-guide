Open your camera at your janamaz (prayer mat). Salah Alarm recognizes it and stops the alarm — proving you got out of bed.

Yes. Salah Alarm uses your precise location and trusted calculation methods for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha.
